About Caves of Qud Wiki

Home / About Caves of Qud Wiki

Caves of Qud Wiki is an unofficial fan-made archive for players who want practical, searchable guidance on Qud builds, mutations, cybernetics, factions, regions, items, creatures and lore.

The site is designed as a static knowledge base: fast, expandable, SEO-friendly and readable on desktop or mobile.

Editorial approach

Articles blend practical strategy with Qud's ancient-machine atmosphere. We prioritize clear survival advice, internal links, source notes and consistent article structure.
