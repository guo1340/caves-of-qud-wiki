# 06-caves-of-qud-wiki.md

# Caves of Qud Wiki Expansion Project

## Project Goal

Transform the Caves of Qud wiki from a collection of informational pages into a strategy-focused player resource that provides unique insights, recommendations, and gameplay expertise.

The objective is not to recreate the official wiki.

The objective is to answer:

- What should I do?
- What should I avoid?
- Which option is better?
- Why do experienced players recommend this?
- What mistakes do new players make?

Google already understands facts.

This wiki should provide interpretation.

---

# Core Content Philosophy

Caves of Qud is a game about:

- exploration
- character builds
- mutations
- progression
- survival
- decision making

Content should focus on:

- strategy
- tradeoffs
- build choices
- player recommendations
- long-term consequences

Avoid writing articles that merely restate game mechanics.

# Content Style Rules

Write as a veteran player.

Avoid:

- encyclopedia tone
- robotic AI wording
- excessive lists
- stat dumping

Prefer:

- recommendations
- analysis
- explanations
- examples

Bad:

"The Double-Muscled mutation increases Strength."

Good:

"Double-Muscled is one of the most reliable physical mutations because it improves combat effectiveness throughout the entire game rather than only during the early stages."

# Community Insight Requirements

Every article must include:

## Community Perspective

Summarize common opinions from:

- Reddit
- Steam discussions
- forums

Examples:

"Many experienced players consider Clairvoyance one of the strongest utility mutations because it improves exploration and survivability at every stage of the game."

"Opinions on Esper builds are divided. Some players view them as extremely powerful while others argue that they require substantial game knowledge."

Never directly quote.

Summarize trends.
